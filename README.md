## Understanding the Five-Layer Construction

Double wall corrugated sheets are made using five paper layers: three flat linerboards and two fluted corrugated mediums. This construction creates a thicker and more rigid board than standard single-wall corrugated material.

The two fluted layers add structural support and cushioning, making the sheets suitable for applications involving heavier products or more demanding handling conditions.

Depending on the required performance, the board can be specified with different paper grades, flute combinations, and sheet dimensions.

## Double Wall vs. Single Wall: When Does the Extra Layer Matter?

Choosing between single-wall and double-wall material depends on the packaging requirement rather than appearance alone.

- **Single-wall sheets:** Contain one fluted medium between two linerboards. They are commonly used for general packaging and lighter applications.
- **Double-wall sheets:** Contain two fluted mediums and three linerboards. They offer greater thickness and rigidity for applications that need additional support.
- **Triple-wall sheets:** Contain three fluted mediums and four linerboards. They are generally considered for more demanding heavy-duty packaging requirements.

For products with greater weight, stacking demands, or handling exposure, double-wall construction may be a more suitable option than single-wall material. The final choice should still account for board grade, dimensions, and actual load conditions.

## Details to Review Before Ordering

A few product specifications can make a significant difference when selecting corrugated sheets for a packaging operation.

- **Sheet dimensions:** Confirm the length and width needed for cartons, pallet layers, or protective panels.
- **Paper grade:** Consider the required strength and the expected handling environment.
- **Flute combination:** Different flute profiles can influence board thickness, cushioning, and rigidity.
- **Load requirements:** Review the weight, shape, and stacking arrangement of the products.
- **Intended application:** Identify whether the sheets will be used for wrapping, separation, pallet protection, or carton conversion.
- **Storage conditions:** Consider exposure to moisture and other environmental factors that may affect paperboard performance.

Providing these details helps narrow down the appropriate corrugated material for the intended use.
